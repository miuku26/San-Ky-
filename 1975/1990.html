At 7 p.m, the whole family turns on the TV.
Our favourite TV show is just starting
Mom heads to the kitchen to prepare dinner
“Come on, you two, help Mom get the bowls and chopsticks ready.”
The rice starts to boil
The smell of food fills the house
“Dinner’s almost ready"
I help my sister set the bowls and chopsticks on the table
Dad turns off the TV
We all gather around the dinner table
Just a simple family meal
But somehow, it feel warm and comforting.
